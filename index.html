The Science Behind GainGuard™

Traditional booster extensions simply multiply the PCM numbers. When audio reaches the digital ceiling (0 dBFS), peaks get chopped into flat square waves. This generates DC heat that cooks speaker voice coils.

  • 55Hz High-Pass Filter: Cuts off physical sub-bass rumble that miniature laptop speakers cannot reproduce.
  • Dynamic Range Compression: Lifts soft details and dialogue by up to 24dB without raising peak ceiling.
  • Hyperbolic Knee Limiter: Smooth continuous saturation replacing harsh digital distortion.
